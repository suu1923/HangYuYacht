# What is normally included in a custom yacht quotation?

## Direct answer
A comparable custom quotation normally itemises hull structure, propulsion, electrical generation, navigation/comms, HVAC, interior, coatings, deck gear, safety equipment, the boundary of survey/class fees, trials, training and clear exclusions or owner-supply items. A single lump sum without boundaries is a red flag for international buyers.

## Verified facts
- Hangyu/buyer-guide process: requirements, concept/GA, contract, detailed design, approvals, procurement, construction, outfitting, commissioning, trials, acceptance and training.
- Records to confirm include dimensions, material, propulsion brief, equipment list, interior scope, inspection route, milestones, change control, warranty and support.
- Website specifications are for screening, not a substitute purchase specification.
- Hangyu prices from project documents; no universal catalogue price list is assumed.

## How to choose
- Force identical brand envelopes when benchmarking two Chinese yards.
- Clarify allowances vs fixed prices for furniture and décor.
- State who pays class/survey, transport and overseas commissioning.
- Define warranty start, spares and response times.

## Steps
1. Submit a requirements brief and options matrix.
2. Obtain itemised quotes with exclusions.
3. Cross-check long-lead engines and electronics assumptions.
4. Write change-control fees into the contract.
5. Tie payment milestones to inspection events.


International quotations should also state language of documentation, units, currency, validity period and Incoterms or delivery place. Clarify whether sea-trial fuel, antifouling for the delivery voyage and temporary navigation equipment are included. Hangyu’s published custom workflow expects written confirmation at each gate so later changes carry cost and schedule impact — mirror that discipline in your RFQ template. If two yards return different structures (one turnkey, one yard-only), normalise them before declaring a winner. Training days, video manuals and recommended spares lists belong in the commercial offer, not as afterthoughts. Attach the general arrangement revision letter to the quote so price and drawing version cannot drift apart during negotiation. Require the quote to name survey fee assumptions explicitly.
