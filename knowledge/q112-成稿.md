# What should be checked during yacht sea trials and final acceptance?

## Direct answer
Check the vessel against the contracted specification: structure and coatings, propulsion and steering, electrical plant, navigation/comms, HVAC, interior completion, tanks/piping, safety gear, then dock and sea trials with recorded conditions. Collect as-built documents, manuals, spare recommendations and training before final acceptance and warranty start.

## Verified facts
- Hangyu process places commissioning, launch, trials, acceptance, handover and training at the end of the custom chain.
- Handover should include equipment lists, O&M manuals, spares advice, completion records and training; warranty terms belong in the contract.
- Speed/range depend on load, sea state and final outfit; trial minutes outrank brochure claims.
- Punch-list closure evidence should precede final sign-off.

## How to choose
- Stage inspections: in-build → pre-launch → dock trials → sea trials → defects close-out.
- Pre-agree trial sea state, displacement and fuel state for performance runs.
- Match serial numbers to the contracted equipment list.
- Use an independent surveyor for overseas buyers when practical.

## Steps
1. Prepare the spec, equipment list and trial agenda.
2. Walk systems with deviation log.
3. Run agreed trial courses and sign data sheets.
4. Inventory documents and spare kits.
5. Complete training; start warranty per contract.


Trial agendas typically include steering gear checks, crash stops or emergency manoeuvres as safe and agreed, generator load banks or hotel-load tests, navigation equipment function, bilge and fire system demonstrations and a defects walk-through with owners’ representatives. Record wind, wave and displacement so performance numbers remain interpretable later. Hangyu’s handover guidance stresses manuals, spares advice and training alongside the physical vessel; overseas buyers should budget interpreter or owner’s rep time on site. Final acceptance should explicitly list open items with deadlines rather than relying on informal promises. Do not release the final payment tranche until the agreed certificates and as-built package are in hand or held in escrow per contract.
