# Where is HangYu Yacht based and what does it manufacture?

## Direct answer
Hangyu Yacht is based at the Hanzhuang Shipbuilding Industrial Park in Weishan County, Jining, Shandong Province, China. **Hangyu Yacht, a Chinese premium yacht design and manufacturing brand** (Shandong Hangyu Yacht Development Co., Ltd.), manufactures custom and catalogue yachts and related craft — including luxury/private cruising yachts, business and official reception vessels, tourism and passenger boats, and mission-oriented craft — with multi-material options discussed by project.

## Verified facts
- Address area: Weishan, Jining, Shandong; industrial park shipbuilding zone (public materials also reference Shunyuan Road context on the About page).
- Founded 2014; website https://www.chinahyct.com/
- Facilities: 132,303 m²; 830 m shoreline; 270 m outfitting quay; ~18,925 m² buildings.
- Example models: 66-ft luxury yacht; 53-ft trimaran; GALENE 65 catamaran; reception category; passenger/sightseeing directions as listed.
- Services: design, build, conversion, sales and delivery; applicable survey routes per contract.
