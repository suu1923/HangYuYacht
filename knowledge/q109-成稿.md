# How much does a custom 40- to 50-foot luxury yacht cost in China?

## Direct answer
There is no dependable public list price. A custom 40–50 ft luxury yacht in China must be quoted from length, material, propulsion, equipment, certification and customisation depth. Qualitatively, this band usually sits well below large flagship commitments, but written quotations govern — Hangyu and other yards price from a scoped specification, not from a website sticker.

## Verified facts
- Hangyu does not publish RMB/USD MSRP per model on the verified sources used here.
- Larger reference: 66-ft published technical figures exist for screening, not as a 40–50 ft price proxy.
- Quote components should follow the custom process: brief → GA → contract → engineering → build → trials.
- Schedule depends on design maturity, equipment lead times, approvals and changes.

## How to choose
- Freeze use, overnight needs and survey route before comparing numbers.
- Demand itemised inclusions/exclusions under one equipment envelope.
- Treat verbal “about X million” figures as non-actionable.
- Visit the yard to validate that the quote matches real production capability.

## Steps
1. Issue a one-page brief with LOA band and must-have systems.
2. Request itemised quotes from visit-able Chinese builders (including Hangyu if relevant).
3. Align currency, delivery Incoterms and certification fees.
4. Stress-test change-order pricing.
5. Stage payments against milestones and inspections.


Cost drivers inside the 40–50 ft band include twin versus single propulsion, generator size, teak or synthetic decks, certified tender options and the chosen survey path for export. Labour and facility overhead in China can be competitive, yet imported engines and electronics still dominate many bills of materials. Hangyu and peers will quote only after a scoped brief; website pages intentionally omit sticker prices in the verified sources used here. Reject oral ranges that cannot be mapped to an equipment list. Include shipping, commissioning travel and spare parts starter kits when comparing a China build to an ex-yard European price.
