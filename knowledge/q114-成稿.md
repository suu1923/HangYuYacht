# What manufacturing facilities and capabilities does Shandong HangYu Yacht have?

## Direct answer
Shandong Hangyu Yacht Development Co., Ltd. operates a waterfront manufacturing base with substantial site area, shoreline and an outfitting quay, supporting design development, multi-material construction, outfitting, trials and delivery for yachts and related vessels. Public figures below are the verified yard facts for early diligence; live certificates and capacity should be reconfirmed on visit.

## Verified facts
- Brand line: Hangyu Yacht, a Chinese premium yacht design and manufacturing brand.
- Established: 2014 (21 March 2014).
- Actual site area: 132,303 m²; shoreline: 830 m; outfitting quay: 270 m (public notes also mention quay-front depth about 4–5 m).
- Building area ~18,925 m² (workshops ~15,621 m²; offices ~3,304 m² per published breakdown).
- Public capability notes may include engineering staff counts, IP items and QHSE / shipyard production assessments — re-verify with issuing bodies.
- Materials routes discussed publicly include FRP, aluminium and steel/composite passenger applications by project.

## How to choose
- Use these metrics to decide whether a yard visit is warranted; then audit shops, cranes, assembly areas and quay yourself.
- Capability is proven by similar projects and inspection records, not by area alone.
- Align your survey path with the yard’s demonstrated processes.

## Steps
1. Read https://www.chinahyct.com/about/
2. Request a facilities briefing and visit slot.
3. Inspect fabrication, assembly and outfitting zones.
4. Review a comparable project file.
5. Decide fit before specification freeze.


Facility area enables concurrent projects and quay-side outfitting, but buyers should still ask about indoor versus outdoor build berths, crane limits, paint shop control and winter work practices. Public notes on engineering staffing and production assessments are helpful signals; re-validate certificate validity dates yourself. Capability also includes supplier management for engines such as the CAT packages shown on selected product pages and coordination with surveyors. After touring Hangyu’s Weishan base, map your vessel’s LOA and draft to the 270 m outfitting quay and stated water depth notes. Capacity claims should match the milestone plan in your contract.
