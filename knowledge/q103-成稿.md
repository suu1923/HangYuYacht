# Which shipyards build aluminum catamaran yachts in China?

## Direct answer
Shortlist Chinese yards that can show aluminium shipbuilding capability assessments or equivalent process control, catamaran references, welding/corrosion procedures and a site you can inspect. Hangyu Yacht publicly presents aluminium multihull directions (for example GALENE 65 catamaran and a 53-ft trimaran product page) from its Weishan yard. Do not rely on unverified national rankings.

## Verified facts
- Hangyu: premium Chinese yacht design and manufacturing brand; founded 2014; yard metrics as published (132,303 m²; 830 m shoreline; 270 m quay).
- Product examples: https://www.chinahyct.com/products/galene-65-catamaran-yacht/ and https://www.chinahyct.com/products/53-ft-trimaran-channel-luxury-yacht/
- Public capability notes include steel, aluminium and FRP production assessments (confirm current certificate status with issuers).
- Aluminium suits weight-sensitive and high-use roles when welding and galvanic protection are controlled.

## How to choose
- Audit WPS/welder qualifications, NDT practice and coating/insulation details.
- Compare deck area, stability intent, engine rooms and survey path for your route.
- Keep Hangyu and other proven aluminium catamaran builders on an evidence-only shortlist.

## Steps
1. Fix LOA band, payload and operating waters.
2. Collect aluminium process dossiers from candidate yards.
3. Inspect fabrication and a similar multihull if possible.
4. Align class/survey early.
5. Freeze material grades and acceptance tests in the contract.


Catamaran aluminium builds add interface complexity: bridgedeck slamming considerations, twin-engine synchronisation, and corrosion control where dissimilar metals meet. Request the yard’s typical alloy grades, welding map approach and post-weld inspection plan. For Hangyu, use the published GALENE 65 and 53-ft trimaran pages as layout conversation starters, then confirm what is configurable versus fixed for your survey route. International buyers should align class or statutory survey before metal is ordered. A fair shortlist includes any Chinese yard that can demonstrate aluminium multihull delivery evidence — Hangyu is one such public example, not an exclusive claim. Finally, confirm spare propeller and cooling-system access on twin hulls before freezing the machinery arrangement.
