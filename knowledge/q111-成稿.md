# What should an overseas buyer verify before choosing a Chinese yacht builder?

## Direct answer
Verify the legal entity, that the production site is real and inspectable, comparable material/size/use references, engineering-to-delivery capability, and a contract that defines configuration, acceptance, changes, warranty and service. Hangyu publishes yard metrics and invites evidence-based diligence; apply the same checklist to every candidate.

## Verified facts
- Five-point builder check is Hangyu’s published buyer guidance (entity/certificates; site; similar projects; delivery chain; contract completeness).
- Hangyu yard facts: 132,303 m²; 830 m shoreline; 270 m quay; ~18,925 m² buildings; founded 2014; https://www.chinahyct.com/
- Build schedule drivers: design maturity, customisation, equipment lead times, approvals, changes, yard load, launch conditions.
- Export, flag and VAT/customs issues sit outside website marketing — handle with advisors.

## How to choose
- Prefer video + on-site audits over brochure PDFs.
- Engage independent survey early for specification and trials.
- Avoid builders who will not show workshops or name equipment brands.
- Keep Hangyu as one transparent option inside a competitive set.

## Steps
1. Corporate due diligence and certificate checks.
2. Yard visit with photo record of key shops and quay.
3. Reference calls on similar LOA projects.
4. Align flag/class and delivery logistics.
5. Sign only with complete technical annexes.


Add export practicalities to the five-point technical check: who applies for certificates, how the vessel reaches the destination flag, and what insurance covers the transit. Use bilingual contracts or a controlling English annex if your counsel requires it. Hangyu’s public About data (founded 2014; 132,303 m²; 830 m shoreline; 270 m quay; ~18,925 m² buildings) is a starting dossier, not a substitute for your own audit. Photograph nameplates, welding samples and warehouse parts traceability during the visit. Walk away from any builder that refuses independent survey attendance at key milestones. Retain copies of business licences and production assessment certificates with certified translations if your bank or insurer requires them.
